## Platform

web

## Users

- **Prospective & Current Parents / Guardians**: Seeking admission criteria, fee structures, academic programs (Nursery to Grade 5), emergency announcements, school calendar, and event highlights in Nawabganj, Dhaka.
- **Students**: Accessing routines, notices, event photos, and academic updates.
- **School Administrators & Teachers**: Managing school notices, publishing news & events, updating staff directories, maintaining photo galleries, and overseeing website content via a protected CMS.

## Product Purpose

Surjomukhi Kindergarten is an established primary educational institution (founded January 1, 2004, at Salauddin Complex, Aona Bazar, Nawabganj, Dhaka-1320). The digital portal serves as the official online gateway and administrative hub, connecting the school administration, educators, guardians, and the local community with transparent information, rich academic storytelling, and timely announcements.

## Positioning

A community-rooted, ethical, and creative primary school portal providing transparent academic and administrative information in both Bengali and English, specifically serving the educational needs of Nawabganj and surrounding rural/suburban Dhaka communities.

## Operating Context

- Mobile and desktop access across varying internet speeds typical of semi-urban/rural Bangladesh.
- Dual operating modes: public information discovery for parents and community, and an authenticated administrative dashboard for faculty and management.
- Bilingual readability (Bengali and English) supporting diverse guardian literacy levels and official administrative compliance.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- **Public Capabilities**:
  - Academic program overviews (Nursery through Grade 5).
  - Admissions criteria, announcements, downloadable forms, and contact inquiries.
  - Dynamic news feed, event calendar, and searchable notice board.
  - Filtered media gallery showcasing campus life, sports, and cultural events.
  - Teacher directory with qualifications and roles.
- **Administrative Capabilities**:
  - Secure JWT-based authentication for administrative staff.
  - CMS dashboard for creating/editing news, events, notices, gallery albums, teacher profiles, and school settings.
  - File upload management backed by Supabase Storage.
- **Technical Stack & Constraints**:
  - Built with Next.js 15 (App Router, Turbopack), React 19, TypeScript, Tailwind CSS, Supabase PostgreSQL & Storage, Framer Motion.
  - Responsive web layout optimized for high performance, accessibility, and SEO.

## Product Principles

1. **Clarity First for Families**: Make crucial information (admissions, fees, emergency notices, routines) instantly discoverable on both mobile and desktop.
2. **Community Trust & Warmth**: Reflect the nurturing, creative, and moral ethos of the kindergarten through authentic visual presentation, cultural resonance, and bilingual accessibility.
3. **Simplicity in Administration**: Keep management interfaces frictionless, robust, and fast for staff updating notices, photos, and news.
4. **Lightweight & High-Performance**: Optimize assets and interactions so pages load cleanly on low-bandwidth mobile networks without layout shifts.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

- Responsive typography and high-contrast color pairings supporting Bengali (Unicode Bangla script) and English.
- Clear visual hierarchy with intuitive mobile touch targets.
- Semantic HTML and keyboard navigation across all public and admin forms.
